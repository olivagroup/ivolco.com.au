Sydney Property Development, From Land to Sale

We find the land, plan the development, manage construction and bring the finished homes to market. IVOLCO is a Sydney-based residential property developer specialising in duplexes and small-scale developments across Greater Sydney. We work with private capital partners, landowners and investors who want to develop property without managing the process themselves.

Why Work With Us

Property development has plenty of ways to go wrong. A site that looks profitable can lose its margin through planning delays, underestimated building costs, financing expenses or a poorly timed sale. Our job is to identify those risks early and manage the project through to completion.

  • Site selection and feasibility: We source and assess land based on development potential, planning constraints, construction costs and local resale demand. Every opportunity is tested against realistic costs, contingencies and market conditions before acquisition.
  • Planning and approvals: We work with planners, designers and consultants to identify a viable approval pathway before committing to a project. Understanding the site's constraints early can avoid expensive redesigns and delays.
  • Construction and cost control: Our established building relationships and detailed pre-construction planning help us establish realistic budgets, reduce avoidable variations and maintain quality throughout construction.
  • One team from start to finish: From acquisition and approvals through construction, subdivision and sale, IVOLCO coordinates the specialists, budgets, timelines and decisions needed to deliver the project.
Our Experience

Established in 2020, IVOLCO has experience across two residential development projects, supported by a builder with a broader construction track record. Our experience includes navigating COVID-era construction disruptions, changing material costs and challenging market conditions. Our latest project in Sydney's Sutherland Shire is available for inspection by arrangement.

Why New Homes Matter

The 2026–27 Federal Budget reforms strengthen the investment appeal of eligible newly built homes. From July 2027, eligible buyers of new residential investment properties can continue to access negative gearing and choose the existing 50% capital gains tax discount rather than the new indexation-based arrangements. For our developments, this is an additional selling point for the finished homes and a consideration in our exit strategy.

Tax treatment depends on the buyer's circumstances and the property's eligibility. Independent tax advice is recommended.
